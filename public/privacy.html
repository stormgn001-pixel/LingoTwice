Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026 • Effective Date: October 31, 2026

At Lingotwice (accessible at https://lingotwice.com), your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Lingotwice, how we use it, and how we protect your personal information in compliance with international privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / CPRA.

Lingotwice is 100% free to use with no paid subscriptions, supported solely by Google AdSense advertising. We do not sell your personal information. Questions? Contact us at support@lingotwice.com.

1. Information We Collect

Lingotwice is designed as an interactive bilingual parallel reading and language learning platform. Depending on how you interact with our service, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Personal Information Voluntarily Provided: When contacting us via our contact form or email at support@lingotwice.com, we collect your name, email address, subject line, and message content.
  • Client-Side Application Data (Local Storage): To provide a personalized reading experience without requiring account creation, Lingotwice stores reading preferences (such as target languages, font size, theme, and reading progress), custom imported texts, and saved vocabulary cards directly in your browser's LocalStorage. This data resides on your own device and is not sold or shared with external parties.
  • Optional Cloud Sync (Firebase): If you sign in with Google, your reading documents, vocabulary cards, preferences, and study statistics are securely synchronized with Firebase Firestore under your user account.
  • Log Files & Technical Identifiers: Like most modern websites, Lingotwice follows standard procedure of using server log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where required by law), browser type, date/time stamps, and referring pages strictly for operational reliability and security.

2. Google AdSense, Cookies & Analytics

To keep all learning tools, translations, and library resources completely free for everyone, Lingotwice displays advertising served by Google AdSense and uses Google Analytics for anonymous web metrics.

  • Google DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads on Lingotwice based on users' visits to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Ad Personalization & Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Consent & Browser Controls: Advertising scripts respect user consent choices and you can disable or manage cookies anytime through your individual browser settings.

3. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the GDPR. Lingotwice aims to take reasonable steps to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of your Personal Data. You have the right to:

  • Right to access: Request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification: Request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
  • Right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to object to processing: Object to our processing of your personal data.
  • Right to data portability: Request that we transfer data that we have collected to another organization or directly to you.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@lingotwice.com.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Rights

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: Lingotwice does not sell consumer personal data.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights.

5. Children's Information Protection

Protecting children's privacy while using the internet is especially important to us. Lingotwice does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as online dictionaries or public domain literature repositories) that are not operated by us. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
